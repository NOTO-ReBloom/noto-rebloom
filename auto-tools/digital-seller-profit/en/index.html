FAQ

Which platform is cheapest?

It depends on price, payment method, buyer country, discovery value, and fulfillment. The lowest fee does not automatically produce the highest profit.

Why are some processor fees not preset?

Processor rates can depend on account country, plan, payment method, and currency. Use the editable extra-processing field for your own conditions.
